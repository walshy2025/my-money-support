My Money SW

My Money SW is a simple personal finance tracker for managing bills, income, account balances and loan repayments.

Privacy

My Money SW does not require an account and does not connect directly to banks or financial institutions.

Financial information entered into the app is stored on the user's device and may be synchronised between the user's Apple devices using their personal iCloud account.

My Money SW does not sell user information or use information entered into the app for advertising.

Financial Disclaimer

My Money SW is a personal financial organisation tool and does not provide financial, investment, credit or lending advice.
